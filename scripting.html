Macro scripts

Click Script in the Macros window to write a macro as text. Each line is one step.

-- Heal, cure and fight
cast "Greater Heal" on self when self.health < 60%
cast "Cure" on target when target.isPoisoned and target.distance <= 10
cast "Energy Bolt" on target when target.isHostile
wait 1.5
say "Next!"
  • -- starts a comment.
  • Case doesn't matter.
  • As you type, a list offers what can come next. Tab or a click takes it.

Actions

Action Does
cast "Spell" Casts a spell.
skill "Skill" Uses a skill.
ability primary Uses your weapon's primary ability.
ability secondary Uses your weapon's secondary ability.
ability "Ability" Uses a racial ability, such as "Stone Form".
do "Action" Does an action from the Add a step list, such as "Toggle War Mode".
use "Item" Uses an item from your pack. For now only "Bandage".
say "words" Says the words. Also emote, whisper and yell.
wait seconds Waits, such as wait 1.5.

Add on self, on target or on cursor to aim cast, skill, do or use.

Conditions

Add when and a condition to run a step only sometimes.

cast "Greater Heal" on self when self.health < 60%
cast "Cure" on self when self.isPoisoned and not self.isHidden
cast "Heal" on target when (target.isFriend or target.isPartyMember) and target.health < 50%
  • Compare numbers with < <= = != >= >.
  • % means a share of the maximum: self.health < 60%.
  • Join tests with and, or, not and brackets.
  • A step whose condition fails is skipped.
  • With no target, every target test fails, except not target.exists.

Tips

  • Use use "Bandage" on self, not do "Bandage Self". Bandage Self ends the macro.
  • A line with a mistake turns red, and the line under the script says what's wrong. The
    macro keeps its last working steps until you fix it.
  • Some steps, such as an equipment set, can't be written as a line. A macro with one stays
    in the steps view.
