Windows

Status

Shows your name, health, mana and stamina.

Opening it

  • It opens when you log in.
  • Use the Status action to open it.
  • Choose Status in your paperdoll's menu to open it.
  • Drag your own character out of the game world to open it.
  • Right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Click the window to target yourself.
  • Drop an item on the window to drop it on your character.
  • Shift+right-click the window to open your character's menu.
