Equipment Positions

Picks which parts of your body a hotbar slot's equipment action works on.

Opening it

  • Shift+right-click a hotbar slot with an equipment action and choose Edit Item.
  • Right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Click a position to choose it for an arm or disarm action.
  • Click positions to tick the ones an unequip action takes off.
  • Click a ticked position to untick it.
