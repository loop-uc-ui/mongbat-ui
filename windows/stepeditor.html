Step Editor

Changes what one step of a macro does and when it runs.

Opening it

  • Click a step in the Macros window to open it.
  • Add a step from the step list to open it on the new step.
  • Right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Click Change to make the step another action.
  • Choose what a step that aims should aim at.
  • Type the words for a step that speaks, or the seconds for a wait.
  • Choose whether the step runs always, when all its tests hold, or when any holds.
  • Set each test to read something about you or your target.
  • Click Add a test to add a test, and the X to remove one.
  • Click Delete to remove the step.
