Step Editor

The Step Editor changes one step of a macro: what it does, and when it runs. It opens beside the Macros window when you click a step there. Every change is made at once.

A step can run always, or only when all of its tests hold, or when any one of them holds. A test reads something about you or your target: a number such as your health, or something that holds or not, such as whether a buff is running or the target is hostile. As the macro runs, each step's tests are checked just before the step, and a step whose tests do not hold is skipped. The tests are the conditions of the script language; the Scripting page describes them.

Opening it

  • Click a step in the Macros window.
  • Adding a step from the step list opens the editor on the new step.
  • To move a step, drag its handle in the Macros window. The editor stays on the step.
  • A right-click closes the window.

Using it

  • Click Change to make the step another action, from the step list. Its tests stay.
  • For a step that aims, choose its target: the cursor, yourself or your current target.
  • For a step that speaks, type its words. For a wait, type how many seconds it waits.
  • Choose when the step runs: always, when all of these hold, or when any of these holds. Choosing all or any gives the step a first test, your health below full.
  • In a test, choose You or Target, then what to read. For a number, choose how it compares and type the value; add % for a share of the maximum, such as 60%. For a skill or a buff, choose which one. For something that holds or not, choose yes or no.
  • Click Add a test for another test, up to four. Click the X beside a test to remove it. With no test left, the step runs always.
  • Click Delete to remove the step.
  • A macro that holds an equip set, an unequip step, a target by resource, or an arm or disarm step cannot be changed here. Drag actions onto it in the Macros window instead.

Changes

v1.0.0-alpha.4
