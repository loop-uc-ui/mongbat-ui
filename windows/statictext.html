Scenery Names

When you click a sign, a banner or another piece of scenery that has a name, its name appears right where you clicked. The name is centred on the click.

Only one name shows at a time. A new click moves the name there and keeps it up a little longer. Scenery that has no name shows nothing.

Opening it

  • The name appears when you click a piece of scenery that has a name.
  • The name goes away by itself after a few seconds. Another click on scenery moves it to the new place and starts the wait again.

Using it

  • The name takes no clicks of its own.
