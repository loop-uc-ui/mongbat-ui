Docks

Shows the creatures near you as health bars.

Opening it

  • Choose Docks in your paperdoll's menu.
  • Use the Docks action to open it.
  • It opens at login if you left it open.
  • Right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Click a tab to see its list.
  • Right-click a tab to filter, add, rename, remove or extract it.
  • Click a bar to target the creature.
  • Control+click a bar to choose your quick-cast spell, and Shift+click to cast it.
  • Double-click a bar to open the creature's paperdoll.
  • Drop an item on a bar to give it to the creature.
  • Shift+right-click a bar to open the creature's menu.
