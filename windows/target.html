Windows

Target

The target window shows what you have targeted. For a creature or a person it draws the same health bar as a parked health bar, with the name in the colour of notoriety, the bar of health, the percent and the distance in tiles. For an item or a corpse it shows the name and the distance, and no bar.

When you target a pet of your own, a row of command buttons stands under the distance. The server's quick actions for other characters, such as banking or taming, appear there too when the server offers them.

Opening it

  • The window opens by itself when you target a creature, a person, an item or a corpse.
  • It closes by itself when you have no target.
  • A right-click does not close it, so that a missed click in a fight cannot hide your target's health.

Using it

  • Click the bar to target the creature again.
  • Control+click the bar to choose the spell that a quick cast puts on the creature. Shift+click it to cast that spell on the creature.
  • Double-click the bar to open the creature's paperdoll.
  • Drop an item on the bar to give it to the creature.
  • Shift+right-click the bar to open the server's menu for the creature.
  • Click a command button to do that action on the target. A button fades while the action is out of reach: banking beyond a short distance, taming beyond a shorter one, or any action the server greys out.
  • Hover over a command button to read its name.
  • Hover over the window while an item or a corpse is targeted to see its properties.
  • Hover over a name that is cut off to read it whole.
  • Drag the window to move it. With the Lock the layout setting on, it stays where it is.
