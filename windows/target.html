Windows

Target

Shows what you have targeted.

Opening it

  • It opens when you target something.
  • It closes when you have no target.

Using it

  • Click the bar to target the creature again.
  • Double-click the bar to open the creature's paperdoll.
  • Drop an item on the bar to give it to the creature.
  • Control+click the bar to choose the spell a quick cast puts on it.
  • Shift+click the bar to cast that spell on it.
  • Shift+right-click the bar to open the server's menu for the creature.
  • Click a command button to do that action on the target.
  • Hover over the window to see a targeted item's properties.
