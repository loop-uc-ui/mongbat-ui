Organizer

Moves items between your open containers by rules.

Opening it

  • Use the Organizer action to open it.
  • Choose Agents Settings in the main menu to open it.
  • Choose Agents in the paperdoll's menu to open it.
  • Click Organize or Restock on a container with nothing to move to open it.
  • Right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Click the profile picker to choose a profile.
  • Click the plus to make a new profile, and the minus to delete one.
  • Click Add item, then target an item to add it to the profile.
  • Click Set bag, then target a container to send items there.
  • Pick Organize or Restock to file items away or restock them.
  • Click Run to start moving items, and Stop to stop.
  • Shift+right-click a rule to remove it or set how many to keep.
