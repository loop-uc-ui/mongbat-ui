Hotbar

Holds actions you use with a click or a key.

Opening it

  • Your hotbars open when you log in.
  • A new character gets a bar of window shortcuts.
  • Shift+right-click the edge of a bar and choose New Hotbar to add one.
  • Shift+right-click the edge of a bar and choose Destroy Hotbar to remove it.

Using it

  • Click a slot, or press its key, to use its action.
  • Drop an action or an item on a slot to put it there.
  • Drag an action from one slot to another to move it.
  • Drag an empty slot to move the whole bar.
  • Hover over a slot to see what it does.
  • Shift+right-click a slot to set its key or what it aims at.
  • Shift+right-click the edge of a bar to name it, colour it, resize it or stand it on end.

Changes

  • Added a red tint to a hotbar slot while its weapon ability is armed.

v1.0.0-alpha.1

  • Your hotbars, key bindings and settings carry over between this UI and the default UI, both ways.
  • Hotbars are moved by dragging an empty slot. A slot shows how many of an item you carry, and counts down the wait after a potion or a bandage.
