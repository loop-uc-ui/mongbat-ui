Tracking Arrow

Points you toward a target, such as a creature you are tracking.

Opening it

  • It appears when you use the Tracking skill, or when a quest points you to a target.
  • It goes away when the server stops pointing you there.

Using it

  • Walk the way the arrow points to reach the target.

Changes

v1.0.0-alpha.1

  • This UI draws its own tracking arrow.
