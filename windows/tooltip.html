Windows

Tooltip

Tells you what the thing under the pointer is.

Opening it

  • Rest the pointer over something to show its tooltip.
  • Move the pointer away to hide it.

Using it

  • Hover over a name that is cut off to read it whole.

Changes

v1.0.0-beta.2

  • Changed scenery that says only its name, such as a tree or a wall, to show no tooltip.

v1.0.0-alpha.4

  • Added a weapon's two abilities to its tooltip, and finding a weapon in a bag by an ability's name.
  • Changed a creature's or a player's name in a tooltip to show in their notoriety colour.
  • Changed tooltips to line an item's values up at the right.
