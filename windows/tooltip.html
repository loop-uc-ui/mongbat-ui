Tooltip

A tooltip is the small box of text that appears when you rest the pointer over something. It tells you what the thing is. Every tooltip is laid out the same way, from the top:

  • the thing's name, in white;
  • what it does;
  • its numbers, one to a line, with each name at the left and its value in white at the right, such as an item's properties or a skill's Real, Modded and Cap;
  • a list, such as a spell's reagents;
  • a hint, dim, such as what a drag does.

A tooltip shows only the parts it has, with a small gap between each two.

The box follows the pointer and stands above and to the left of it. At the top or left edge of the screen, it moves to the other side so that it stays whole. A tooltip too tall for the screen shortens what it says the thing does, and ends it with "...".

Opening it

  • The tooltip appears when you rest the pointer over something that has a tooltip, such as a buff, a spell, an item, a hotbar slot or a button.
  • An item's tooltip appears once the server has sent the item's properties. Until then, nothing shows.
  • The tooltip goes away as soon as the pointer leaves the thing, or when the thing closes.

Using it

  • Rest the pointer over a thing to read its tooltip. Nothing needs a click.
  • Move the pointer, and the tooltip moves with it.
  • Hover over a row whose name is cut off to read the name whole.
