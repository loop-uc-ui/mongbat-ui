Step List

The step list holds every action that a macro step can be, in groups, with a Search box under the title to find one by name. It opens from the Macros window, and a click adds the action as a step.

Opening it

  • Click Add a step in the Macros window.
  • Click Change in the Step Editor, to make that step another action.
  • A right-click closes the window.

Using it

  • Type in the Search box under the title to show only the actions whose names match.
  • The groups are the actions as the Actions window groups them, then your skills, the spells of each school, and your abilities.
  • Click an action to add it after the step the Step Editor has open, or at the end of the macro. The Step Editor then opens on the new step, so the next click adds after it.
  • An item is not in the list. Drag it from a bag onto the macro instead.
  • Use the button at the top right to switch between a list and a grid of pictures.
