Damage Numbers

A damage number shows how much damage a hit did. It appears on the creature or player that was hit and floats upward, fading as it rises.

The colour says who was hit. Red is a hit on you, pink is a hit on one of your pets, and yellow is a hit on anyone else. The number follows the creature while it rises, so it moves with the creature as the creature moves.

Opening it

  • A number appears whenever the game reports that someone in view took damage.
  • Each number goes away by itself when it has risen far enough. A crowded fight keeps only the most recent numbers.

Using it

  • Damage numbers take no clicks.
