Distance Counter

Shows how many tiles away the pointer is while you choose a target.

Opening it

  • It shows when a spell, a skill or an item asks for a target.
  • It goes away when you choose the target or cancel.

Using it

  • Point at a creature to see how far away it is.
  • Point at the ground to see how far away that spot is.
  • Aim a spell that hits an area to see the tiles it will reach.
  • Aim a field spell to see the tiles the field will cover.

Changes
