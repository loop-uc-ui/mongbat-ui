Distance Counter

The distance counter shows how many tiles away the place under your pointer is, while the game waits for you to choose a target. It stands just below and to the right of the pointer, on a small plate, and it counts tiles the way the game counts range: the larger of the steps east or west and the steps north or south.

Over a creature it shows the game's own distance to that creature. Over the ground it works the distance out from where the pointer stands, and it follows the camera's zoom.

Opening it

  • The counter appears by itself whenever a spell, a skill or an item asks you to choose a target, and the pointer is over the world.
  • It goes away when you choose the target or cancel it, and while the pointer is outside the world's frame.

Using it

  • The counter takes no clicks. A click goes to whatever is under it.
  • The game does not tell the UI how far the camera is zoomed. The counter reads it from where the game draws a point that it pins over your character, so the count follows the zoom as you change it. Each creature you point at checks that reading.
  • Over the ground the count assumes level ground. A hill or a roof can put it a tile out. Over a creature the count is always exact.
