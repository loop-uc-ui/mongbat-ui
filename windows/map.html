Windows

Map

Shows the world around you.

Opening it

  • Press the Toggle Map key to open it.
  • Use the Atlas action to open it.
  • It opens at login if you left it open.
  • Right-click the map to close it.

Using it

  • Drag the map to look around.
  • Choose Center on Player in the map's menu to follow you again.
  • Roll the mouse wheel to zoom in and out.
  • Double-click a place to centre the map there.
  • Hover over a marker to read its name.
  • Click the three dots to open the map's menu.
  • Shift+right-click a place and choose Create Waypoint Here to mark it.
  • Shift+right-click your own waypoint to rename, change or delete it.
