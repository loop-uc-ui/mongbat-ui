Quantity

Asks how many items to take from a stack.

Opening it

  • It opens when you drag from a stack.
  • Click Cancel or right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Type a number in the box.
  • Drag the slider to choose an amount.
  • Click Okay or press Enter to take that many.
