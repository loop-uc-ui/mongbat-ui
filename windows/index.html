Windows

Every window of the UI, each drawn without the game client. A picture shows the window in one state, with made-up names and items.

Actions

Lists the actions you can put on a hotbar or in a macro.

Alert

Warns you in red at the top of the screen when you are in danger.

Book

Lets you read a book, and write in one that is yours.

Buffs

Shows the buffs and debuffs on your character.

Change Race

Lets you choose a new look when you use a race-change token.

Character Sheet

Lists your stats.

Chat

Shows everything said around you and to you.

Chat Filters

Hides chat messages or puts your own words in their place.

Chat Line

The box you type in to speak.

Confirmation

Asks before something hard to undo, or tells you something you must read.

Container

Shows what is inside a bag, a box or a corpse.

Damage Numbers

Shows how much damage each hit did.

Debug Log

Shows the client's log inside the game.

Distance Counter

Shows how many tiles away the pointer is while you choose a target.

Docks

Shows the creatures near you as health bars.

Dragged Item

Shows what you are carrying on the cursor.

Dye Colors

Picks the colour a dye tub dyes an item.

Equip Items

Edits the set of items a hotbar slot puts on.

Equipment Positions

Picks which parts of your body a hotbar slot's equipment action works on.

Find a Place

Finds a place by name and moves the map to it.

First Run Questions

Asks how to set up a character that is new to this client.

Harvest With

Sets what a hotbar slot's tool harvests.

Health Bar

Shows one creature's health, wherever you drop it.

Hotbar

Holds actions you use with a click or a key.

House Customization

Lists the pieces you can build with while you edit a house.

Ignored Players

Holds the players you don't want to hear.

Jewelry Box

Shows everything in a jewelry box at once.

Key Prompt

Asks you to press the key you want for an action.

Macros

Lets you make and edit your macros.

Map

Shows the world around you.

Map Legend

Picks which kinds of marker the map draws.

Menu

A short list of choices that opens beside the pointer.

Name Plates

Shows the names of the items and creatures near you.

Names and Speech Over Heads

Shows names and speech over the heads of people and creatures.

Organizer

Moves items between your open containers by rules.

Paperdoll

Shows what a character is wearing.

Party

Shows everyone in your party and their health.

Party Invite

Asks whether you want to join a party.

Picture Picker

Lets you choose a picture for a macro or a map marker.

Quantity

Asks how many items to take from a stack.

Quick Stat

Keeps one stat from the character sheet on the screen.

Scenery Names

Shows the name of a piece of scenery you click.

Server Dialogs

Shows a window the game server sends, such as a travel menu or a runebook.

Shop

Buys from a vendor or sells to one.

Skill Tracker

Keeps your skills in view while you play.

Skills

Lists every skill your character has.

Spellbook

Shows the spells in one spellbook.

Status

Shows your name, health, mana and stamina.

Step Editor

Changes what one step of a macro does and when it runs.

Step List

Lists every action a macro step can be.

Submenu

A group of choices that opens beside a row of a menu.

Target

Shows what you have targeted.

Text Prompt

Asks you to type an answer, such as a name or a number.

Tooltip

Tells you what the thing under the pointer is.

Tracking Arrow

Points you toward a target, such as a creature you are tracking.

Trade

Swaps items and gold with another player.

User Settings

Changes how the game and this UI behave.

Waypoints

Lists the places you marked on the map.
