Party

Shows everyone in your party and their health.

Opening it

  • It opens when you join a party, and closes when the party ends.
  • Use the Party action to show the bar again while you are in a party.
  • Right-click the bar to close it.

Using it

  • Click a member to target them.
  • Double-click a member to open their paperdoll.
  • Drop an item on a member to give it to them.
  • Control+click a member to choose the spell a quick cast puts on them.
  • Shift+click a member to cast that spell on them.
  • Shift+right-click a member to open the party commands.
