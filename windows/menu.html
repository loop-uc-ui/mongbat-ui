Menu

A menu is a short list of choices that opens beside the pointer. Each row is one choice. A tick at the right of a row marks the choice that is in force, a dimmed row is not available now, and an arrow says that the row opens a group of more choices beside it. A thin line above a row starts a new group of rows.

Many parts of the UI use this one menu: the hotbar, the chat, the map, the skills list, the paperdoll, the main menu, a macro's steps, and the menus that the server offers for a pet. What the rows say depends on what opened the menu.

Opening it

  • Shift+right-click a hotbar slot for the slot's menu, or the edge of a hotbar for the bar's menu.
  • Shift+right-click a step in the Macros window for the step's menu.
  • Shift+right-click a skill in the skills list for the skill's menu.
  • Click the three dots at the left end of the chat's typing line for the chat's menu.
  • Click the three dots on the world map for the map's menu, or Shift+right-click the map window outside its picture. Click the three dots on your own paperdoll for the paperdoll's menu.
  • Click a drop-down box, such as the profile picker in the Organizer, to open its list under the box. A list of more than ten rows scrolls.
  • Click the sort button of the skills list, or the storey visibility button of the house designer, for their menus.
  • Use the Main Menu action, from the Actions window or a hotbar, for the main menu.
  • Right-click a pet in the world for the server's menu for it.
  • The menu opens beside the pointer. Near an edge of the screen, it moves back so that it stays whole.
  • Choosing a row closes the menu. A click anywhere else closes it too.

Using it

  • Click a row to do what it says. The menu closes first, then the choice is carried out.
  • Hover over a row with an arrow to open its group beside it. Hover over a row without an arrow to close the group.
  • A dimmed row does nothing when clicked.
  • Roll the mouse wheel over a long list to scroll it.
