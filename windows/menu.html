Windows

Menu

A short list of choices that opens beside the pointer.

Opening it

  • Shift+right-click a hotbar slot to open the slot's menu.
  • Shift+right-click the edge of a hotbar to open the bar's menu.
  • Shift+right-click a step in the Macros window to open the step's menu.
  • Shift+right-click a skill in the skills list to open the skill's menu.
  • Click the three dots on the world map to open the map's menu.
  • Click the three dots on your own paperdoll to open the paperdoll's menu.
  • Click a drop-down box to open its list.
  • Click the sort button of the skills list to open its menu.
  • Click the storey visibility button in the house designer to open its menu.
  • Use the Main Menu action to open the main menu.
  • Right-click a pet to open the server's menu for it.
  • Choose a row or click anywhere else to close the menu.

Using it

  • Click a row to choose it.
  • Hover over a row to open its group, if it has one.
  • A dimmed row can't be chosen.
