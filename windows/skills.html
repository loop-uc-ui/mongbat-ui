Windows

Skills

Lists every skill your character has.

Opening it

  • Press the Skills Window key to open it.
  • Use the Skills action to open it.
  • Choose Skills in your paperdoll's menu to open it.
  • Right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Type in the Search box to filter the list.
  • Click the sort button to change the order.
  • Click a skill's lock to change it.
  • Click the three dots to set every lock at once.
  • Double-click a skill to use it.
  • Drag a skill onto a hotbar to keep it there.
  • Shift+right-click a skill to open its menu.
  • Choose Add to Favorites in a skill's menu to keep it at the top of the list, under Favorites.

Changes

v1.0.0-beta.1

  • Added Favorites to the Skills, Spellbook, Actions and Character Sheet windows, to keep what you use most at the top of the list.
  • Changed long lists, such as the Step List, to open faster.

v1.0.0-alpha.5

  • Changed Skills, spellbooks and Actions to show a list only, and the Jewelry Box a grid only.
  • Changed skills, spells and stats to show no tooltip.

v1.0.0-alpha.4

  • Added and, or, not, brackets and quoted phrases to every find box.
  • Changed every find box to stand under its window's title, and every place to type to stand on a dark plate.

v1.0.0-alpha.3

  • A hover over a skill or a stat says what it is.

v1.0.0-alpha.1

  • The Skills window has a find box and shows a list or a grid of pictures. A skill can be given an auto-lock value.
