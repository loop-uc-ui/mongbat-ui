Dye Colors

The dye palette opens when you use a dye tub on an item. It is a grid of colour swatches, from the brightest shades at the top to the greyest at the bottom, and the colour you click is the colour the item is dyed.

Opening it

  • The palette opens when you use a dye tub on an item and the server asks for a colour.
  • It closes when you click a swatch or Cancel.

Using it

  • Click a swatch to choose that colour. The palette closes and the item is dyed.
  • Click Cancel to close the palette without dyeing anything.
  • Drag the palette to move it.
