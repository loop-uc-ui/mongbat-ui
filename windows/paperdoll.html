Paperdoll

Shows what a character is wearing.

Opening it

  • Press the Character Window key to open your own paperdoll.
  • Use the Paperdoll action to open your own paperdoll.
  • Double-click another character to open theirs.
  • Double-click a member in the party bar to open theirs.
  • Right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Hover over a piece of gear to see its properties.
  • Double-click a piece of gear to use it.
  • Drag gear off the paperdoll to take it off, or onto it to wear it.
  • Click the view switch to change between the gear view and the picture view.
  • Click the three dots for a menu of your windows.
  • Choose Rewards in that menu to see your veteran rewards, when you have some to claim.
  • Click the war square to switch between war and peace.
  • Double-click an ability square to make the ability ready.
  • Drag an ability square onto a hotbar to keep it there.
  • Click the backpack square to open the backpack.
  • Shift+right-click a piece of gear to open its menu.
