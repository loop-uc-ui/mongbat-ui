Spellbook

Shows the spells in one spellbook.

Opening it

  • Double-click a spellbook to open it.
  • Right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Type in the Search box to filter the spells.
  • Click the sort button to change the order.
  • Double-click a spell to cast it.
  • Drag a spell onto a hotbar to keep it there.
  • Shift+right-click a spell to open its menu.
  • Choose Add to Favorites in a spell's menu to keep it at the top of its book, under Favorites.
  • A Book of Masteries lists each skill's masteries under the skill's name. The skill whose mastery you chose is at the top, marked Active Mastery, with a gold star.
