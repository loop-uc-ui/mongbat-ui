Spellbook

A spellbook window shows the spells of one book, such as a book of Magery, Necromancy or Chivalry. Each row shows the spell's picture and name and what it costs to cast, such as the skill it needs and its mana. A book of Chivalry also shows tithing points, and the foot of the book shows how many tithing points you have.

A spell that the book does not hold yet is faded, and it cannot be cast or picked up.

Opening it

  • Double-click a spellbook to open it. The window opens beside the pointer.
  • A right-click anywhere on the window closes it.

Using it

  • Type in the Search box under the title to show only the spells whose names match. Press Escape to empty the box.
  • Click the sort button to choose an order: by name, or by the costs the book shows, such as skill, mana or tithing. Each order runs upward or downward, and the book remembers your choice. A book of masteries has no sort button.
  • Click the view switch to change between the list and a grid of pictures. The book remembers your choice.
  • Double-click a spell to cast it.
  • Drag a spell onto a hotbar to keep it there.
  • Hover over a spell to read what it does and what reagents it needs.
  • Roll the mouse wheel over the list to scroll it.
  • Drag the corner grip to resize the window.
  • Right-click to close the window.
