Submenu

A submenu is a group of choices that flies out beside a row of a menu. It opens when the pointer rests on a row that has an arrow at its right end, and it closes when the pointer moves to a row that has no arrow. Each row is one choice, and a tick marks the choice that is in force.

A submenu opens on the right of its row. When the menu stands too near the right edge of the screen for that, it opens on the left. When the menu stands near the bottom, the group stands up from its row.

Opening it

  • Hover over a row that has an arrow in any menu. The group opens beside the row at once.
  • Moving the pointer to another row with an arrow replaces the group with that row's group.
  • Moving the pointer to a row without an arrow closes the group.
  • Choosing a row of the group closes the whole menu. A click anywhere else closes it too.

Using it

  • Click a row of the group to choose it. The menu closes, and the choice is carried out.
  • Move the pointer along the group's rows to read them. Moving the pointer within the group keeps it open.
