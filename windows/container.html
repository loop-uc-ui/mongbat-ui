Windows

Container

A container window shows what is inside a backpack, a bag, a box or a corpse. Each item is a small picture in a grid of cells, and a stack of items shows its count under the picture. The grid has a cell for every slot the bag has, and each item stays in the slot where you put it, with empty slots between. The bag's name stands at the top, and under it a Search box where you can type to search the bag. The lower right corner shows how many items the bag holds, counting those in the bags inside it, and the most it can hold, such as 12/125.

Every container you open gets a window of its own. The windows open a step apart, so that each one leaves the title of the one beneath it in reach. Your backpack opens where you last left it. The other containers share one size, which is the size you last dragged a container to.

Opening it

  • Double-click a bag, a box or a corpse in the world, or in another container, and the server opens it.
  • The backpack key in the client's key settings opens your backpack, and closes it again.
  • The Backpack action, in the Menu Actions group of the Actions window, does the same. You can put the action on a hotbar.
  • The freeform view button closes the window and opens the bag again in the freeform view.
  • A right-click on the window's padding or footer closes it. A right-click on an item closes the window too.

Using it

  • Double-click an item to use it. Double-click a bag to open it in a window of its own.
  • Press on an item and drag it to pick it up. Release it on an empty slot to put it in that slot, or on an item or elsewhere on the window to drop it into this container.
  • Control+press on a stack and drag to take one item from it.
  • Drag the window by its name or its padding to move it. Hover over a name that is cut short to read it whole. With the Lock the layout setting on, it stays where it is.
  • Roll the mouse wheel over the window to scroll the rows.
  • Drag the scroll bar to move through the rows.
  • Hover over an item to read its name and its properties. A weapon's hover also names its primary and secondary abilities.
  • Type in the Search box to show only the items that match. Words are matched against item names and properties, and a weapon's abilities, and every word has to match. Put or or | between words to accept either one. Put -, ! or not before a word to leave out items that match it. Brackets group words, and quotes hold an exact phrase.
  • While the box holds a search, the matches stand together from the first cell. A search also looks inside the bags in this container, by item names and weapons' abilities. Matches found there join the end of the list, and the box's tooltip names the bags that hold them.
  • Press Escape in the box to empty it and show everything again.
  • Click Loot All on a corpse to move its items into your backpack one at a time. It becomes Stop while it works, and a second click stops it. When the box holds a search, the button reads Loot with the number of matches, and only the matches move.
  • On a container that is not a corpse, the footer button appears only once the box holds a search. It reads Take with the number of matches, and it moves them into your backpack. Your own backpack never shows it.
  • Click Organize to move items by the rules of your chosen profile in the Organizer window. When the profile has nothing to move, the click opens the Organizer window instead. The button reads Restock when that profile fetches items, and Stop while the work goes on.
  • Click the button at the top right to switch between the grid and the freeform view, when the bag has a picture of its own to draw. Hover over it to see which view it leads to.
  • Drag the corner grip to make the grid wider, narrower, taller or shorter. Every container then opens at that size.
  • Shift+right-click an item on a corpse to loot it at once. On other containers it opens the item's menu.
  • Shift+right-click the window's padding or footer to open a menu with Narrower and Wider, which change the number of columns.
  • Right-click to close the window.

Changes

v1.0.0-alpha.4

  • Added and, or, not, brackets and quoted phrases to every find box.
  • Added a weapon's two abilities to its tooltip, and finding a weapon in a bag by an ability's name.
  • Changed every find box to stand under its window's title, and every place to type to stand on a dark plate.
  • Changed a large container, such as a corpse that others are looting, to do much less work as it opens and as items leave it.
  • Fixed a bag's item count staying at the old number after the server moved an item out.

v1.0.0-alpha.3

  • A container keeps each item in its own slot, as the default UI left it, and an item let go on an empty slot lands in that slot. Before, items were packed in order, and a used-up stack moved every item after it.
  • The container's View button shows a pouch for the freeform view.

v1.0.0-alpha.2

  • A container shows in its lower right corner how many items it holds, counting those in the bags inside it, and the most it can hold.

v1.0.0-alpha.1

  • Containers show a grid of items or the bag's own picture. A find box searches the bag and the bags inside it, and a corpse has Loot All.
