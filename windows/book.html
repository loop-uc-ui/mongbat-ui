Book

Lets you read a book, and write in one that is yours.

Opening it

  • Double-click a book to open it.
  • Right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Click Next to turn forward two pages.
  • Click Previous to turn back two pages.
  • Click Edit in your own book to write on the pages.
  • Click Okay to send what you wrote.

Changes
