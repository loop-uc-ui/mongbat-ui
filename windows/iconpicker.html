Picture Picker

Lets you choose a picture for a macro or a map marker.

Opening it

  • Click the picture at the top of the Macros window to open it.
  • Shift+right-click one of your own waypoints and choose Waypoint Icon.
  • Right-click the picker to close it without choosing.

Using it

  • Click a picture to choose it.
  • Drag the corner of the grid to change its size.
  • Hover over a picture in the list to read its name.

Changes
