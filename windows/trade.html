Trade

Swaps items and gold with another player.

Opening it

  • It opens when you or another player start a trade.
  • Click Refuse to end the trade and close it.

Using it

  • Drop an item on the window to offer it.
  • Click Offer gold or Offer plat to offer an amount.
  • Double-click a bag on either side to look inside it.
  • Hover over an item to see its properties.
  • Click Accept to accept the trade.
  • Click Cancel to take your acceptance back.
