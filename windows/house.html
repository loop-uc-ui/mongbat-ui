House Customization

Lists the pieces you can build with while you edit a house.

Opening it

  • Start customizing a house to open it.
  • Right-click an empty part of the window to close it and stop editing.

Using it

  • Click a tab to see that kind of piece.
  • Right-click a tab and choose Extract to give it a window of its own.
  • Click a piece, then click in the world to place it.
  • Click Erase, then click a piece in the world to remove it.
  • Click Eyedropper Tool, then click a piece in the world to choose it.
  • Click a storey number to build on that storey.
  • Click the Story Visibility button to show, fade or hide the walls, floors or roofs of a storey.
  • Click + or - beside Roof to set the roof's height.
  • Click Backup, Restore, Sync, Clear or Revert at the foot to change the whole design.
  • Hover over a button at the foot to read what it does.
  • Click COMMIT to build the design.
