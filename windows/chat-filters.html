Chat Filters

Hides chat messages or puts your own words in their place.

Opening it

  • Click Chat Filters on the Chat page of the settings.
  • Use the Chat Filters action to open it.
  • Type /chatfilters in chat and press Enter.
  • Right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Type in the Add a filter box to add a filter.
  • Type the words to match, with the same case and punctuation.
  • Choose Contains to match the words anywhere, or Exact to match the whole message.
  • Choose Hide to hide the message, or Replace to show your own words instead.
  • Drag a filter's handle to move it up or down.
  • Click the cross to remove a filter.

Changes
