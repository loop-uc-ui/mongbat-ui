Alert

Warns you in red at the top of the screen when you are in danger.

Opening it

  • The banner shows by itself when something is wrong.
  • Use the Alert action to turn the banner on.
  • Use the Alert action again to turn it off.

Using it

  • Read the banner to see your two most urgent warnings.
  • Click through the banner to reach the world under it.

Changes
