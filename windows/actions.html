Windows

Actions

Lists the actions you can put on a hotbar or in a macro.

Opening it

  • Choose Actions in the main menu to open it.
  • Choose Actions in your paperdoll's menu to open it.
  • Use the Actions action to open it.
  • Click Actions at the bottom of the Macros window to open it.
  • Right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Drag an action onto a hotbar slot to put it there.
  • Drag an action onto the next step of a macro to add it.
  • Double-click a menu action to run it.
  • Type in the search box to filter the list.
  • Shift+right-click an action to open its menu.
  • Choose Add to Favorites in an action's menu to keep it at the top of the list, under Favorites.

Changes

v1.0.0-beta.1

  • Added Favorites to the Skills, Spellbook, Actions and Character Sheet windows, to keep what you use most at the top of the list.
  • Added Rewards to your paperdoll's menu, a Rewards action and /rewards, to open your veteran rewards.
  • Changed the headings in lists to the usual colour with a line after them.
  • Changed long lists, such as the Step List, to open faster.
  • Changed the Jewelry Box, Actions and Step List windows so you can drag them from the space between their entries.

v1.0.0-alpha.5

  • Changed Skills, spellbooks and Actions to show a list only, and the Jewelry Box a grid only.

v1.0.0-alpha.4

  • Added and, or, not, brackets and quoted phrases to every find box.
  • Changed every find box to stand under its window's title, and every place to type to stand on a dark plate.
  • Changed the Actions window's grid to leave a gap under each heading.

v1.0.0-alpha.1

  • The Actions window lists every action in groups, as rows or as a grid of pictures.
