Windows

Character Sheet

Lists your stats.

Opening it

  • Click the three dots on your paperdoll and choose Character.
  • Use the Character sheet action to open it.
  • Right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Type in the search box to filter the stats.
  • Click the lock on Strength, Dexterity or Intelligence to make it rise, fall or stay.
  • Control+click a stat to show or hide it as a small readout on the screen.
  • Shift+right-click a stat to open its menu.
  • Choose Add to Favorites in a stat's menu to keep it at the top of the sheet, under Favorites.

Changes

v1.0.0-beta.1

  • Added Favorites to the Skills, Spellbook, Actions and Character Sheet windows, to keep what you use most at the top of the list.
  • Changed the headings in lists to the usual colour with a line after them.

v1.0.0-alpha.5

  • Changed skills, spells and stats to show no tooltip.

v1.0.0-alpha.4

  • Added and, or, not, brackets and quoted phrases to every find box.
  • Changed every find box to stand under its window's title, and every place to type to stand on a dark plate.

v1.0.0-alpha.3

  • The character sheet shows Hit Points, Stamina and Mana at the top, each with what your gear adds, as in 90 / 100 (+5). The rows that read such as 0 / 5 are gone.
  • The character sheet's stat cap counts only your trained stats. Before, what gear and buffs add made it read over the cap.
  • A hover over a skill or a stat says what it is.

v1.0.0-alpha.1

  • The character sheet lists your stats in four groups under a find box, and a stat can be shown on screen as a Quick Stat.
