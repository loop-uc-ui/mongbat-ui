Character Sheet

The character sheet lists your stats, one row to a stat, in four groups: Character, Attack, Magic and Defense. Each row shows the stat's picture, its name and its current value, such as your strength, your gold, your weight or a resistance.

Strength, Dexterity and Intelligence also carry a lock at the right end of the row. Your weight is drawn in red when you carry more than you can bear.

Opening it

  • The Character sheet action, in the Menu Actions group of the Actions window, opens or closes the sheet. You can put the action on a hotbar.
  • On your own paperdoll, click the three dots at the top right and choose Character.
  • A right-click anywhere on the window closes it.

Using it

  • Type in the box at the top to show only the stats whose names match. Press Escape to empty the box.
  • Click the lock on Strength, Dexterity or Intelligence to change it. Each click moves it to the next of three states: up, down and locked. You can also click anywhere on the row.
  • Control+click a stat to put that stat on the screen as a small readout of its own. Control+click it again to take the readout away. See the Quick Stat page.
  • Hover over a stat whose name is cut off to read it whole.
  • Roll the mouse wheel over the list to scroll it.
  • Drag the window to move it.
  • Drag the corner grip to resize the window.
  • Right-click to close the window.
