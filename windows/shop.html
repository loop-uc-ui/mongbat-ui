Windows

Shop

Buys from a vendor or sells to one.

Opening it

  • It opens when a vendor offers to buy or sell.
  • Click Cancel or right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Click an item in the left list to add one to your order.
  • Click an item in your order to take one off.
  • Shift+right-click an item in either list to take one off.
  • Click the # button on a row to type how many you want.
  • Type in the Search box to filter the left list.
  • Click Accept to send your order.
  • Shift+right-click the heading to fill your order from an Agents profile.
