Party Invite

The party invite asks whether you want to join a party. It says who invited you, as "Mara Ashdown invites you to join the party.", with Accept and Decline under the words.

Opening it

  • The invite opens in the middle of the screen when another player invites you to a party.
  • Accept joins the party and closes the invite.
  • Decline refuses and closes the invite. If the invite goes away in any other way while it is still waiting, it counts as a refusal.

Using it

  • Click Accept to join the party.
  • Click Decline to refuse.
  • Drag the invite to move it out of the way.
