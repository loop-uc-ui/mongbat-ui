Party Invite

Asks whether you want to join a party.

Opening it

  • It opens when another player invites you to a party.
  • Click Accept or Decline to close it.

Using it

  • Click Accept to join the party.
  • Click Decline to refuse.

Changes

v1.0.0-alpha.4

  • Changed a dialog's buttons to stand at the lower right, with the button that agrees on the right.
  • Fixed a party invite staying up after the invite ended.
