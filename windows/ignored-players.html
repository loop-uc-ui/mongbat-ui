Ignored Players

Holds the players you don't want to hear.

Opening it

  • Click Ignored Players on the Chat page of the settings.
  • Use the Ignored Players action to open it.
  • Type /ignored in chat and press Enter.
  • Right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Click Add a player under a list to add someone.
  • Choose Add By Target and click a player to add them.
  • Choose a name from the people who spoke in channel chat to add them.
  • Click the cross beside a person to stop ignoring them.
