Chat

Shows everything said around you and to you.

Opening it

  • The chat opens when you enter the game.
  • Press the chat key to show the chat.
  • Use the Chat log action to bring the chat back.
  • Use the Chat log action again to put it away.

Using it

  • Click a tab to show it.
  • Right-click a tab to pick its channels, or to add, rename, remove or extract tabs.
  • Click a line to target the speaker.
  • Double-click a line to open the speaker's paperdoll.
  • Shift+click a line to pull out the speaker's health bar.
  • Shift+right-click a line to open the speaker's menu.
