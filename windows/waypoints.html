Waypoints

Lists the places you marked on the map.

Opening it

  • Choose Waypoints in the map's menu to open it.
  • Use the Waypoints action to open it.
  • Right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Click a row to move the map to that waypoint.
  • Shift+right-click a row to rename, change or delete the waypoint.
  • Click Create Waypoint Here to mark where you stand.
  • Shift+right-click Create Waypoint Here to mark a place by its sextant reading.

Changes

v1.0.0-alpha.4

  • Fixed renaming a map marker leaving a copy when the map changed world meanwhile, or removing the marker when the new one was refused.

v1.0.0-alpha.1

  • The map lists only your own waypoints, and has a find box and a legend.
