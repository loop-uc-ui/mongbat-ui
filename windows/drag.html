Dragged Item

Shows what you are carrying on the cursor.

Opening it

  • Pick up an item or an action to show it.
  • Drop what you carry to make it go away.

Using it

  • Move the mouse to carry the item where you want it.
  • Drop it on a window, a slot or the ground to put it there.
