Dragged Item

The dragged item is the picture that follows your cursor while you carry something. It shows the item you picked up, or the action you picked up from a list or a hotbar. When you carry several of an item, such as part of a stack, its count stands beside the picture.

Opening it

  • It appears when you pick up an item or an action.
  • It goes away when you drop what you carry or put it back.

Using it

  • Move the mouse to carry the picture where you want it. It hangs from the cursor by its top-left corner.
  • Drop it on a window, a slot or the ground to put the item or action there.
  • It takes no clicks of its own.
