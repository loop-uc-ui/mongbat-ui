Change Race

Lets you choose a new look when you use a race-change token.

Opening it

  • Use a race-change token to open it.
  • Click Cancel or right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Click a colour swatch to choose a colour.
  • Pick a style from its list to change it.
  • Click Okay to change to the look you chose.
