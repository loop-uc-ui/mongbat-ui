Quick Stat

Keeps one stat from the character sheet on the screen.

Opening it

  • Control+click a stat in the Character Sheet to make a readout.
  • Right-click a readout to remove it.

Using it

  • Drag a readout to move it.
  • Shift+right-click a readout to open its menu.
  • Choose Rename to give the readout your own name.
  • Choose Set Amount Warning to turn the readout red below a percent.
  • Choose Set Value Color to change the readout's colour.
  • Choose Destroy Label to remove the readout.
