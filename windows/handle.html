Name Plates

A name plate is a small label that floats over an item or a creature near you and tells you its name. Plates appear over nearby objects while you hold the key for object handles, so that you can find and pick out things in a crowded place. The client's settings choose which kinds of object get a plate, in Object Handle Filter, and how many plates show at once, in Object Handle Quantity. Both are on the Overhead page of the settings, and the filter can also be chosen from the list that shows with the plates.

The name on a plate is in the colour of the object's notoriety. A name that is too long for the widest plate is cut at a word and ends in dots, and a hover shows all of it.

Opening it

  • Hold Ctrl and Shift together. A plate appears over each object near you that the filter and the quantity allow.
  • The plates go away when the client takes them down again.
  • Right-click a plate to close that plate alone.

Choosing the filter

  • While the plates show, a list of the filters stands at the top of the screen, with the filter in use lit. Drag it to move it, and it opens where you left it.
  • Turn the wheel anywhere on the screen to move up and down the list, or click a filter. The plates change at once to show what the filter allows.
  • Besides the game's filters, the list has Item Only (items that are not corpses), Hostile (creatures with a grey, orange or red name), Friends (green names and your party) and My pets.
  • Let go of Ctrl and Shift, and the list goes with the plates. The filter you chose is saved, and the next time you hold the keys it is the one in use.
  • While the list shows, the wheel moves the list and does not turn the camera.
  • The default UI gets the filter you chose here when you switch back to it. Hostile, Friends and My pets are this UI's own, so the default UI shows every plate for them.

Using it

  • Click a creature's plate to target the creature. Shift+click casts your quick-cast spell on it, and Control+click chooses that spell.
  • Drag a creature's plate to pull off a health bar for the creature and drop it where you want it.
  • Click an item's plate to target the item, and drag the plate to pick the item up.
  • While the cursor waits for a target, a click on a plate answers it with that object.
  • Double-click a plate to use the object.
  • Hover over a plate to see the object's properties. A hover shows nothing when the properties would only repeat the name on the plate. The plate brightens and rises over its neighbours.

Changes

v1.0.0-alpha.4

  • Changed a creature's or a player's name in a tooltip to show in their notoriety colour.
  • Changed the name plates' filter list to look like a menu, under the title Object Handles.

v1.0.0-alpha.3

  • While you hold Ctrl and Shift for the name plates, a list of filters shows with them. Turn the wheel anywhere, or click a filter, and the plates change at once. Drag the list to move it, and it opens where you left it. Your choice is saved when you let go.
  • The name plate filters add Item Only, Hostile, Friends and My pets. The default UI gets the filter you chose when you go back to it, or every plate for a filter it does not have.
  • A name plate's hover no longer shows only the name that is already on the plate.
