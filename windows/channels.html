Windows

Channels

The Channels window lists the chat channels that the server offers and shows the one you are in. The title names your channel after the word CHANNELS. A tick stands at the right end of the row of the channel you are in.

You can be in one channel at a time. Joining another leaves the last one.

Opening it

  • The Channels action, in the Menu Actions group of the Actions window, opens the window and closes it again. You can put the action on a hotbar.
  • The Channels entry in the chat menu opens it too. The chat menu opens from the button on the chat line, and from Shift+right-click on the chat.
  • The window closes when you open it again from either, and a right-click anywhere on the window closes it.

Using it

  • Double-click a channel to join it. Double-clicking the channel you are already in does nothing.
  • Hover over a channel to read what a double-click will do.
  • Click Leave to come out of your channel. The button shows only while you are in one.
  • Click Create to type a name for a new channel. A blank name is not sent.
  • Roll the mouse wheel over the list to scroll it.
  • Drag the corner grip to resize the window.
  • Right-click to close the window.
