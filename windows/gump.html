Windows

Server Dialogs

Shows a window the game server sends, such as a travel menu or a runebook.

Opening it

  • A dialog opens when the server sends one.
  • The Town Crier's greeting does not open when you log in.
  • The veteran rewards do not open when you log in. Choose Rewards in your paperdoll's menu, use the Rewards action, or type /rewards to open them.
  • Right-click the veteran rewards to put them away until you open them again.
  • Use a runebook to open its dialog.
  • Right-click the dialog to close it without choosing.

Using it

  • Click a button to give that answer.
  • Click Next or Previous to see more choices.
  • Type your answer in the box and press Enter to send it.
  • Click Submit to send your answer, Clear to empty the box, or Cancel to send nothing.
  • Click a rune in a runebook to choose it.
  • Click a travel button in a runebook to travel to the chosen rune.
  • Click the three dots in a runebook for more things to do with the rune and the book.
