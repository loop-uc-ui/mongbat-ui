Buffs

Shows the buffs and debuffs on your character.

Opening it

  • Click the three dots on your paperdoll and choose Buffs.
  • Use the Buffs action to open it.
  • It opens at login if you left it open.
  • Right-click the bar to close it.

Using it

  • Hover over an effect to read what it does.
  • Look at the end of a row to see how long the effect has left.

Changes
