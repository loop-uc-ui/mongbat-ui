Jewelry Box

Shows everything in a jewelry box at once.

Opening it

  • Double-click a jewelry box to open it.
  • Right-click the window to close it.

Using it

  • Hover over a piece to see its properties.
  • Click a piece to move it to your backpack.
  • Type in the search box to filter the grid.
  • Click the plus to add jewelry.
  • Drag a piece onto the window to add it.
