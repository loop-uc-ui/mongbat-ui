Jewelry Box

The Jewelry Box window shows every piece in a jewelry box at once, in place of the box's own pages of fifty. The pieces stand under a heading for each kind: rings, bracelets, earrings, necklaces and talismans. A Search box under the title narrows them by their properties.

The box sends its pieces a page at a time, so the window reads every page when it opens, and again when the box changes. The box does not send what a piece looks like, so each piece is drawn with the plain picture of its kind. Hover over a piece to see what it is.

Opening it

  • Double-click a jewelry box to open the window in place of the box's own dialog.
  • Close the window with a right-click, or walk away from the box.

Using it

  • Hover over a piece to see its name and properties.
  • Click a piece to take it out to your backpack.
  • Type in the find box to show only the pieces that match, as a bag's find box does. A kind's name finds its pieces, so "ring fire" finds the rings with fire on them. Press Escape to empty the box.
  • Click the plus to add jewelry, then click each piece to add. Press Escape when you are done.
  • Drag a piece from your backpack onto the window to add it.
  • Click the button beside the find box to switch between the grid and a list with names. The window remembers your choice.
  • Roll the mouse wheel to scroll the window, and drag the corner grip to resize it.
