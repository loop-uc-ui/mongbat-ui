Health Bar

Shows one creature's health, wherever you drop it.

Opening it

  • Drag a creature out of the world to pull out its health bar.
  • Drag a creature's name plate to pull out its health bar.
  • Hold Shift and drag a box over the ground to pull out a bar for every creature inside it.
  • Right-click a bar to close it.
  • Use the Close all health bars action to close every bar.

Using it

  • Click a bar to target the creature.
  • Double-click a bar to open the creature's paperdoll.
  • Control+click a bar to choose the spell a quick cast puts on the creature.
  • Shift+click a bar to cast that spell on the creature.
  • Drop an item on a bar to give it to the creature.
  • Shift+right-click a bar to open the server's menu for the creature.
  • Drag a bar to move it.
